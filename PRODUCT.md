# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: recruiters and hiring managers reviewing Lorenz Tazan for infrastructure-oriented technical roles in a constrained job market.
- Secondary: engineering leaders and technical interviewers who need enough depth to assess systems, platform, DevOps, SRE, cloud, network, automation, and security-adjacent experience.

## Product Purpose

Help Lorenz secure a new technical role by making his fit understandable quickly while giving technical reviewers a credible path into project details, operational evidence, and measurable outcomes.

## Positioning

Position Lorenz under a flexible infrastructure and systems umbrella rather than locking the portfolio to one narrow title. Role-specific language may map that core to IT Support, Systems Engineer, Platform Engineer, DevOps, Cloud Infrastructure, or Network roles when the evidence supports it.

The portfolio must support hands-on IT Support, Service Desk, Desktop Support, and IT Technician applications. These paths foreground troubleshooting, setup, user communication, documentation, and escalation before advanced scale or ownership. Breadth belongs in explicit role pathways and evidence filters, not in a long multi-title headline.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from a job application, recruiter search, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a shared résumé. Recruiters need a fast qualification scan; technical reviewers may continue into projects, architecture, implementation details, and source repositories.

Lorenz is intentionally considering roles at different seniority levels in the current job market. The experience adapts its evidence order to the visitor's target role without hiding chronology or changing factual titles. It does not steer him toward inbound customer-call work as a default path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve factual claims, experience, contact details, résumé access, project links, and measurable outcomes unless Lorenz explicitly approves a content change.
- Keep the public experience fast, responsive, accessible, and useful without requiring sign-in.
- Use Astro as a static-first application deployed on Cloudflare Workers.
- Do not invent employers, responsibilities, project evidence, credentials, screenshots, diagrams, testimonials, or performance metrics.
- Do not publish private job-search context, immigration history, compensation comparisons, location, or personal circumstances.
- Use "clients" or "clients/customers" for Teknomahika without a numerical lifetime total. Use a verified project or infrastructure metric only in its correct scope and only when it improves role fit.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Lorenz Tazan.
- Maintain a direct, technically credible, professional voice.
- Present AI as part of infrastructure automation work, not as a substitute for systems engineering fundamentals.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified résumé source notes and public work history.
- Public repository documentation for sanitized architecture and portfolio labs.
- Existing public portfolio at `lorenztazan.com` and source repository at `AIKUSAN/portfolio`.
- No professional portrait, original project screenshots, or verified production architecture-diagram asset set has been confirmed for the redesign.

## Product Principles

- Make role fit legible in seconds without presenting Lorenz as unfocused.
- Let visitors choose IT Support, Systems & Network, or Cloud & Platform while keeping one canonical profile and URL structure.
- Lead with operational evidence and outcomes, then reveal technical depth progressively.
- Keep entry-level pathways hands-on and approachable; reserve senior architecture and scale signals for visitors who deliberately open deeper evidence.
- Prefer real artifacts and verifiable project evidence over decorative technology imagery.
- Preserve a clear path to résumé download, project inspection, GitHub, and contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The public portfolio remains usable across desktop and mobile, with keyboard navigation, readable contrast, visible focus, semantic structure, and reduced-motion behavior.
